# Code of Conduct

Beeblio welcomes people who use, report issues in, and contribute to the project. Participate with respect for different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent, and correct mistakes without personal attacks.
- Respect others' privacy and do not post their private data or credentials.
- Credit others' work and disclose relevant interests when recommending a provider or product.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and reporting

This applies to Beeblio issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces. If conduct concerns arise, email the maintainer at [mail@beeblio.org](mailto:mail@beeblio.org). Do not post personal details in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports, protect reporters' privacy as far as practical, and may edit or remove content or restrict participation. The maintainer is also subject to this policy.
